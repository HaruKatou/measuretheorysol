\begin{exercise}
show that every compact convex polytope in \(\mathbb R^d\) is Jordan measurable
\end{exercise}
Remind that on compact metric space, a compact set is set close and bounded

\begin{proof}
If \(A\) empty, it measurable and measure = 0.

Suppose \(A\) not empty.
Since \(A\) is compact on \(\mathbb R^d\), then exist \(b > 0\) such that 
\(A \subset [-b, b]^d\). 

A is formed by intersecting of finitely half space \(H_i := \{x \in \mathbb R^d: x.v \leq c\}\). And \(C_i := B \cap H_i\) is measurable.
\(A\) is finite intersection of \(C_i\) then \(A\) is measurable.
\end{proof}
